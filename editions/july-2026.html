APP

Dictation & Financial Tools: Parrot (Mac) & Quartr (iPhone)

For the more tech savvy audience, the Mac app is a dictation app available through GitHub. It maps to your fn button and inserts whatever you say to wherever your cursor is. Might sound like it is not useful, but try it.

The iPhone app is a financial reporting app. If you are ever interested in the financial reports of a company, the app has transcripts, audio, PDFs etc. of the reports. The most useful feature is a calendar of when the reports are coming, so you know when there will be stock movements for a certain company.
