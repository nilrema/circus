APP · MAC / IPHONE

PlainWalk (iPhone) & Unwall (Mac)

iPhone: PlainWalk. Mac: Unwall. PlainWalk is a clean, Japanese-designed walking tracker that turns simple daily steps into a little doodle of a map (and animates it for you). For Mac, I will cheekely pass a webpage this time. Unwall is a lightweight web tool to get past paywall on sites such as Medium, NYT, and such.
