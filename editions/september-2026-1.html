APP

Compression & Sunlight Tracking: Clop (Mac) & Sun Day Tracker (iPhone)

On Mac, Clop is an intelligent image compression tool. Drop images and watch it optimize them without losing quality—simple, fast, and actually useful.

On iPhone, Sun Day Tracker is an app made by Jack Dorsey (the founder of Twitter). It helps you track daylight hours and measure the amount of vitamin D intake in your day (one of the most important things is getting enough sunlight!!!)

Revolut Confirms Sensitive Customer Data Breach

The fintech company disclosed a significant data breach affecting customer information after falling for fake government requests. Another reminder that no company, no matter how tech-forward, is immune to social engineering. Another reminder that KYC laws easily backfire, and often times do not work at all.
