The Circus — #06 — China, brain rot, and regrets of the dying

EDITION No. 06 · OCTOBER 2

October, part two. The same sea of noise, with a few more things worth fishing out.

LONG READ

How China became rich

A piece on how China went from poor to rich, and what actually drove the growth. Notice how their governments are run like businesses.

BOOK READ

Top Five Regrets of the Dying — Bronnie Ware

A palliative nurse's account of what people wished they had done differently at the end of their lives. The book gets too spirital near the end and that's generally not my cup of tea, but it's still a good read. Alternatively, just google the top five things.

WATCH

How to Avoid AI Brain Rot

A talk by Miles Cranmer on using AI to extend your thinking instead of replacing it. This one is for Mateo.

APP · MAC / IPHONE

Google AI Edge Foresight (Mac) & Crumbs (iPhone)

Mac: Google AI Edge Foresight. A Mac app from Google that uses its model to locally transcribe audio. Horrible naming, though. iPhone: Crumbs: Spasi hranu. A local app that helps rescue surplus food from restaurants and bakeries across Zagreb at a discount before it goes to waste.

NEWS

The Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2026 — Press release

A lot to choose from this month. The “protests” in Milan/Paris, politics etc. My lady told me about this, though. This year's prize goes to work on using light to manipulate neurons in the brain.

ZAGREB

Ing-Grad and Allegheny Park Residence build a new tower complex

Ing-Grad has agreed the key terms of a roughly €50 million contract to build the first tower of the Allegheny Park residential complex in Zagreb. The renders look nice, the number of planned apartments is absurd. These are the same investors who are building then new complex near Remiza.

CROATIA

Rož 100: pizza, kava, automobili

A veeery cool pizzeria and coffee spot near Opatija that doubles as a showroom for sexy cars.

TECH

Flock is using trademark law to silence critics

FIRE on how Flock Safety, the licence-plate-reader company, has been using trademark complaints against people who criticise or map its cameras. Comically ironic - we can map you, but you cannot map us.

MISC

J.K. Rowling follow-up essay

J.K. Rowling's has been uniquely positioned to defend the right to freedom of speech. I am glad she is also uniquely positioned to defend women's rights, too.
